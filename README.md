#  ChurnScope
### Customer Behavior • Churn Patterns • Retention Insights • Revenue Risk

> Turning customer data into a visual story that helps explain why customers leave.

ChurnScope is an advanced Customer Churn Analytics and Data Visualization project built with Python. The project explores customer behavior, identifies important churn patterns, compares customer segments, and transforms data into clear business-oriented insights through advanced and interactive visualizations.

Rather than focusing only on numbers, ChurnScope focuses on the story behind the data — who is leaving, what patterns are visible, which customer groups show higher churn, and what revenue is associated with customer churn.

---

##  Project Objective

Customer churn can directly affect revenue, retention, and long-term business growth.

The objective of ChurnScope is to analyze customer data and create a comprehensive visual narrative that explores questions such as:

- Which customer groups show higher churn?
- How does customer tenure relate to churn?
- How do different contract types compare in terms of churn?
- How do monthly charges differ between churned and retained customers?
- Which payment methods show different churn patterns?
- Which customer segments require closer retention analysis?
- What monthly revenue is associated with churned customers?
- What meaningful business insights can be derived from the data?

##  Visualization Story

The project follows a question-driven visualization approach instead of creating charts randomly.

### 01 — Customer Churn Overview

Understand the overall distribution between retained and churned customers.

### 02 — Contract & Churn

Explore how different contract structures relate to observed churn patterns.

### 03 — Tenure & Customer Retention

Analyze how customer tenure differs between churned and retained customers.

### 04 — Monthly Charges & Churn

Compare monthly charge distributions between churned and retained customers.

### 05 — Service & Churn Patterns

Explore how different service combinations relate to customer churn.

### 06 — Payment Method Analysis

Compare churn patterns across different payment methods.

### 07 — Interactive Customer Risk Map

Use an interactive Plotly visualization to explore customers across:

**Tenure × Monthly Charges × Churn**

### 08 — Customer Risk Matrix

Combine tenure and monthly charges to identify customer groups that deserve closer attention.

### 09 — Customer Segmentation

Group customers into meaningful behavioral segments and compare their churn patterns.

### 10 — Revenue at Risk

Translate customer churn into a business-oriented revenue perspective.
